Challenge 4 of the Hack2Skill GenAI Hackathon asked us to pick one vertical for the FIFA World Cup 2026.

I picked the fan experience — then realised the same live crowd feed that rescues a lost fan is exactly what the people running the stadium are flying without.

So I built both, from one engine: a multilingual fan concierge, and an organizer operations dashboard.
